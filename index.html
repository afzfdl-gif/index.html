FAQ

Before you ask

 

What do I need to send?

Your raw footage (phone footage is fine), your logo/handle if you want it burned in, and one line about the video’s goal. We handle everything else.

Which platforms do you edit for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts — plus 16:9 cuts for long-form. Every delivery includes the ratios your package covers.

How fast is delivery?

48 hours standard on monthly plans, 72 hours for single videos, 24 hours priority on Studio. Rush slots open weekly — ask on WhatsApp.

Can you match my current style?

Yes. Send 2–3 reference videos you like (yours or anyone’s) and the first draft follows that language — then we sharpen it.
